Getting started

Installing P.S. Shelf

Add it from the Chrome Web Store
Click "Add to Chrome" on the P.S. Shelf listing, then confirm in the popup that appears.
Pin it to your toolbar (optional but handy)
Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find P.S. Shelf, and click the pin icon next to it so it's always one click away.
Open Pinterest
Go to pinterest.com. Click the P.S. Shelf toolbar icon to open the sidebar — it slides in on the right side of the page.
Connect your Pinterest account (optional)
In the sidebar's ⚙ Settings tab, click "Connect Pinterest" to sync your real boards. You can also just start saving pins locally without connecting anything.
Start saving
Hover any pin on Pinterest — a small "P.S." button and board shortcuts appear. Click to save.
The sidebar

What each tab does

📌
Shelf
Your main feed — every pin you've saved, newest first.
  • Search across saved pins, notes, and tags at once
  • Star a pin as a favorite
  • Edit a pin's note, tags, price, or section
  • Assign or move a pin to a board
  • Open the original pin on Pinterest, or remove it from your Shelf
🧹
Pins
A cleanup view for your whole saved collection.
  • Overview stats: total pins saved, duplicates found, pins with no board
  • Dedicated Duplicates view to spot and remove repeats
  • Dedicated No Board view for pins you haven't organized yet
📋
Boards
Every board you have, with a real read on how alive it actually is.
  • Automatic Active / Dormant / Inactive status, based on real save activity
  • Manually mark any board active or inactive yourself
  • Set and track a budget per board
  • View a board's sections
  • Import your existing Pinterest pins from a board into the Shelf
  • Create new boards, or group boards together into a Box
🖼️
Collage
Build a custom moodboard from pins across any of your boards.
  • Pull pins from one or more boards into a single custom layout
  • Arrange and export the result
📦
Boxes
A layer above boards, for grouping related ones together.
  • Group multiple boards under one Box (e.g. a season, project, or theme)
  • Boxed boards stay out of your main Boards list so it doesn't get cluttered
  • Export a Box as a PDF or image set
📊
Stats
A visual read on your whole collection.
  • Board health breakdown — how many boards are active, dormant, or inactive
  • Budget vs. actual spend across all your priced pins
  • Save activity over the last 8 weeks
  • Your top boards by pin count, and top sections
⚙
Settings
Account, plan, and connection management.
  • Connect or disconnect your Pinterest account
  • See your current plan (Free or Pro) and what each includes
  • Activate a Pro license key
  • See how many devices your license is active on, and deactivate one if needed
  • Optional Google sign-in for cross-device cloud sync (Pro)
